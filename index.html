How guests opt in to receive a text

Last updated: October 3, 2026

The NAHSE 2026 Photo Booth is an iPad kiosk at the NAHSE National Conference 2026 in Los Angeles, California, operated by Juan Clark. The only text message it sends is a guest's own photo, sent once, to the number that guest types in at the booth.

Opt-in steps

  1. The guest takes a photo at the booth.
  2. The booth shows the photo with a phone number field, a keypad, and the consent statement below.
  3. The guest types their own mobile number and taps Send Photo. Tapping the button is the opt-in.
  4. One picture message containing that photo is sent to that number. No further messages are sent.
