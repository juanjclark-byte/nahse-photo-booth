Program

NAHSE 2026 Photo Booth, operated by Juan Clark. Guests at the NAHSE National Conference 2026 in Los Angeles, California can take a photo at the booth and have it sent to their mobile phone as a picture message.

How you opt in

You opt in by typing your own mobile number on the booth screen and tapping "Send Photo". By doing so you agree to receive one text message containing your photo. Only enter a number that belongs to you.

Message frequency

One message per photo you ask to have sent. There are no recurring messages.

Cost

The booth is free to use. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages from this number. Reply HELP for help, or email juanjclark@gmail.com.

Delivery

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery depends on your carrier and device and is not guaranteed.

Your photo

Photos are sent only to the number entered at the booth. Please be considerate of others who appear in your photo.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy for how your phone number and photo are handled.
