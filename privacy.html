Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy covers the NAHSE 2026 Photo Booth, an iPad photo booth at the NAHSE National Conference 2026 in Los Angeles, California, operated by Juan Clark ("we").

What we collect

  • Your photo, when you take one at the booth.
  • Your mobile phone number, only if you type it in to have the photo texted to you.

How we use it

We use your phone number for one purpose: to send you a single text message containing your photo. We do not send marketing or recurring messages.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. Your number and photo are passed only to our messaging provider (Twilio) and mobile carriers, solely to deliver your message.

We do not sell personal information.

Retention

  • The booth does not save your phone number after your message is sent.
  • Your photo is held temporarily so it can be delivered and is deleted from the booth's server within 24 hours. During that time it can be opened only through the private link shown to you at the booth (as a QR code) or attached to your text message.
  • Our messaging provider keeps delivery records, including the destination number, under its own retention policies.

Your choices

Texting is optional; you can use the booth without entering a number. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help. To ask a question or request deletion of information related to you, email juanjclark@gmail.com.

Children

The booth is intended for conference attendees and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
